PEDAGOGY & LEARNING DESIGN

“No Memorization Without Understanding”

Rote learning fails under O/L examination pressure. My classroom structure transforms textbook chapters into living mental models through memory techniques, step-by-step schematics, and interactive reasoning.

Foundation

Intuitive Sinhala Grammar

Sinhala grammar (ව්‍යාකරණ) is often dreaded by middle schoolers. I decompose Sandhi (සන්ධි), Samasa (සමාස), and Vibhakti (විභක්ති) using rhythmic tables and formulaic rules so students apply them automatically in essay composition.

Key Outcome:
Zero marks lost in Part I 40-question MCQ papers.

Perspective

Relatable Buddhist Philosophy

Instead of dry facts, Buddhism is discussed as an ethical and analytical guide for modern adolescent life. Students remember stanzas (ගාථා) with deep contextual meaning, historical events, and Sri Lankan Buddhist heritage effortlessly.

Key Outcome:
Structured 5-point essay answers scoring top rubric marks.
