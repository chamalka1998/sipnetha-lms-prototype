Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Are lessons conducted strictly following the government textbook? expand_more

Yes, 100%. Every single chapter in the official National Institute of Education (NIE) curriculum is thoroughly covered line-by-line. In addition, we enhance textbook content with past paper analyses and provincial question patterns so students aren't surprised by novel term test questions.

How is Geography map marking taught online? expand_more

We provide high-resolution printable blank map sets via PDF prior to the class. During live Zoom sessions, Teacher Chamodya utilizes an overhead document camera and dual screen projection to show exact millimeter plotting, mountain ranges, and river paths in real-time. Students submit photos of their marked maps for individual feedback.

What if my child misses a live Zoom class? expand_more

All live sessions are uploaded within 4 hours to the student LMS portal with complete 1080p video clarity. Students have unlimited replay access until their final term examination concludes, ensuring zero disruption due to school events or connectivity issues.

How do you assess student Sinhala essays? expand_more

Students write timed weekly essays on printed templates and upload scanned copies. Teacher Chamodya personally marks grammar mistakes (අක්ෂර වින්‍යාසය, ලිංග භේදය, ප්‍රත්‍ය දෝෂ) and awards scores matching the official O/L marking rubric: Structure (10), Content (15), and Language/Spelling (15).
